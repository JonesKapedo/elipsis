Incident communication

  • Urgent incidents are acknowledged on the status page within 30 minutes.
  • We update at least every 60 minutes while an urgent incident is active.
  • Every urgent incident gets a written postmortem within 5 business days.
  • We state what we know, what we do not know and when the next update is due.

Service credits

If monthly availability falls below 99.9%, a paid account can request a credit: 10% below 99.9%, 25% below 99.5% and 50% below 99.0%. Credits apply to the next renewal and are not cash refunds. Request them through support within 30 days of the affected month.

Escalation

If a ticket misses its target, reply with “escalate” or ask for the founder. Escalations are answered within 4 business hours. A missed target is not a reason to stop communicating.

Exclusions

The targets do not cover outages caused by customer systems, misuse, force majeure, or scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance. Announced maintenance is excluded from availability only when it stays inside the published window.

How we measure

Support metrics come from ticket timestamps and due times. Availability is computed from public incident records over a rolling 30 days. We publish the live number on the status page rather than asking you to trust a quarterly summary.
