3. Scoring pipeline

A

Evidence-adjusted answers

Scores are tempered by evidence strength so “we think so” does not equal “we can show it”.

B

Subdomain → pillar roll-up

Weighted means by subdomain, then by pillar, then into the {{ INDEX_SHORT }}.

C

Declarative metrics

36 formulas evaluated by a restricted AST interpreter — no black-box model weights.

D

Pain → roadmap

Low scores trigger pain points; recommendations are phased by complexity and ROI.

4. Financial model

Annual savings are sized from staff count, blended hourly rate, recoverable friction, and a conservative ceiling on the labour line so board packs stay credible.

  • Hours × rate driven by organisation size
  • Severity scales with low subdomain scores
  • Recoverable value capped (default 12% of labour line)
  • Investment and payback months printed with assumptions
