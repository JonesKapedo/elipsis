Refund policy

Clear rules for digital reports, facilitated reviews, and Operator passes. If something is wrong, tell us first — we will fix it or refund it.

Version 2026-11-01.

1. Constraint Report — $499

  • Full refund within 14 days if the report has not been generated or downloaded.
  • No refund after the report is generated or downloaded. It is a digital deliverable produced for your inputs, and access is perpetual.
  • One free re-run if an answer was entered incorrectly or a department was selected by mistake. Ask within 30 days.

2. Facilitated Report — $1,900

  • The report component follows the Constraint Report rules above.
  • Cancel at least 48 hours before the session for a full refund, provided the revised report has not been delivered.
  • One free reschedule at any time. Cancelling inside 48 hours, or after the session, is not refundable.
  • If we cancel and cannot offer a workable alternative, you receive a full refund.

3. Operator — $129/month or $999/year

  • Operator is either a one-time pass or, where checkout shows a renewal plan, a subscription. Cancel a renewal anytime from billing; access runs to the end of the paid period.
  • If a renewal was charged in error, contact us within 14 days for a refund of that renewal, provided the new period has not been used.
  • We do not pro-rate part-used periods. Access continues to the end of the paid period.

4. Duplicate or unauthorised charges

Duplicate charges, charges after a failed cancellation, and unauthorised card use are refunded in full. Contact us before raising a chargeback so we can resolve it faster; we may pause paid features while a chargeback is investigated.

5. How to request a refund

Use support and include the payment reference from your billing history. We aim to decide within 5 business days. Approved refunds return to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Any tax charged is refunded where required by law.

6. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under mandatory consumer law in your jurisdiction. Where those rights give you a stronger remedy, they apply.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The version that applied when you paid governs that purchase. Material changes are announced on the changelog.
