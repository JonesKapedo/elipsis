Change notice

We publish a changelog entry and email paid customers at least 30 days before a new subprocessor starts handling customer data. If you object on reasonable data-protection grounds, contact us and we will discuss an alternative or a termination path.

What is not a subprocessor

A provider that only receives aggregate, non-identifying traffic data, or that never receives customer content, is listed only when it can materially affect confidentiality. We still document it in the security review.
