Questions buyers actually ask

Why is the one-time assessment cheaper than a subscription?

Because it covers one assessment and three introductions. A subscription is for teams that re-score regularly and need unlimited implementation switches.

What happens after three introductions?

You can buy a top-up for three more at half the one-time assessment price. Subscribers do not need top-ups.

Do you take a cut of implementation work?

No. Partners pay a subscription for verified access. Elipsis does not charge a commission on the work you agree with them.

What if the diagnosis finds little?

Then you have saved the cost of a consulting engagement. The free diagnostic is designed to answer that question before you pay.
