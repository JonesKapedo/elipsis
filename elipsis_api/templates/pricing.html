01 / PRICE

Why does the report cost $499?

It replaces a week of on-site discovery with a structured document: costed exposure, assumptions, a 90-day plan, and a board pack. Comparable diagnostics often start at $2,000+.

02 / FREE

What stays free?

Your index, maturity band, confidence, the names of your top three constraints, and one sample action. No card required.

03 / COMMISSION

Do you take a cut of implementation work?

No. Partners pay a subscription for the workspace. Elipsis does not charge a commission on the work you agree with them.

04 / REFUNDS

What if the report is not right?

Full refund within 14 days if the report has not been generated or downloaded. After delivery, we will re-run it once at no charge. Full refund policy.
