FAQ

Common questions about Elipsis

What is the {{ INDEX_NAME }}?

A weighted score (0–100) across seven transformation pillars. It summarises readiness for automation and digital change in a single number boards can track.

How long does an assessment take?

About 25 minutes for {{ total_questions or 63 }} plain-English questions in short segments. Answers autosave; you can resume later.

Can I share a report without giving someone a login?

Yes. From any completed results page, create a share link. Anyone with the URL can view a read-only pack — ideal for emailing a board pack.

What is department comparison?

When several units in the same organisation complete assessments, Compare ranks them on the index, maturity, savings, and a pillar heatmap.

Is the financial model audited?

No. Figures are management estimates from staff count, rates, and friction severity, with a conservative savings ceiling. Assumptions are printed so stakeholders can challenge them.

How do I try without filling 63 questions?

Use the public sample report, or sign in and run the guided demo which pre-fills multi-department answers.
