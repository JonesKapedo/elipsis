Our target

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA for public pages, signup, the assessment, results, company workspace, support and settings. WCAG 2.2 became a W3C Recommendation on 12 December 2024.

What we have done

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels and a visible keyboard focus style.
  • Text alternatives for brand and interface imagery where the image carries meaning.
  • Reduced-motion support and no essential information conveyed by colour alone.
  • Form labels, error text and status messages that can be announced by assistive technology.
  • Responsive layouts that reflow on narrow screens and support zoom.
  • Plain-language error messages that say what to fix.

Known limitations

  • Some legacy tables and charts are not yet fully optimised for narrow screens or screen-reader navigation.
  • The generated PDF report is designed for print and may not preserve a logical reading order in every PDF reader.
  • Third-party payment pages are controlled by Paystack and are outside our direct codebase.
  • We have not completed an independent external audit. We do not claim one.

Testing

Each release should check keyboard-only navigation, focus order, zoom at 200%, reduced motion, form labels and error announcements. Automated checks are useful but do not replace a manual pass.

Report an access blocker

If a page blocks you, email {{ support_email }} or open a support ticket. Include the page, the task, your browser and the assistive technology you use. We treat an access blocker as a service defect, not a feature request.

Feedback and enforcement

We acknowledge accessibility reports within 2 business days and aim to give a fix or a workaround within 10 business days. If you are not satisfied, ask for escalation to the founder through the support ticket.

Need a different format?

We can provide report content in an alternative format on request. Ask support and tell us what you need.
