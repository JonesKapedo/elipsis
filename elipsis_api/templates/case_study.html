The pilot protocol

  1. Baseline: one organisation, 3–5 departments, 63 questions each, evidence labels recorded.
  2. Validation: finance lead challenges staff count, hourly rate and savings cap; assumptions are frozen for the pilot.
  3. Plan: three 30-day actions with named owners.
  4. Re-assessment: repeat after 90 days; report movement and what actually changed.

What we will publish

Baseline and follow-up index, the three actions taken, verified savings or hours released, and what did not work. Client name only with written consent; otherwise anonymised by sector and size.

Illustrative pattern — not a customer result

A 40-person logistics firm with fragmented dispatch and manual proof-of-delivery typically scores in the low 40s. A credible first wave is process standardisation and exception tracking, not a large software purchase. The model may show a five-figure annual labour opportunity, but that figure must be validated against payroll before it is presented as savings.

This paragraph is a worked example to show the shape of a report. It is not a claim about a named client or a guarantee of results.
