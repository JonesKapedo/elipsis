Friction cost is not a line item on the P&L — it is the annualised cost of manual workarounds, rework, wait states, and under-used capacity that the assessment signals expose. The model converts those signals into {{ CURRENCY }} using stated headcount, a blended labour rate, and a conservative savings ceiling.
