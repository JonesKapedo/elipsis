Executive readout

Northline is not losing margin because demand is weak. It is losing margin in the handoff between sales, dispatch, and the dock. The operating model depends on re-keyed orders and informal escalation. That shows up as idle time, rework, and a cash cycle that is longer than the operating rhythm should require.

The first move is not a new platform. It is a single dispatch handoff standard with an owner, a timestamp, and a daily exception review. The 90-day plan sequences that before warehouse and finance changes so the business can see movement early.
